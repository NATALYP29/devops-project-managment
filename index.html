About Loved My Pets

We rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals from our local community. Our team of volunteers and staff provide medical care, behavior support, and adoption services.

Our Mission

To provide loving shelter, medical care, and adoption services so every pet has a chance for a safe home.

What We Do

  • Rescue and medical care
  • Behavioral assessments and training
  • Community outreach & education
  • Volunteer and foster programs
